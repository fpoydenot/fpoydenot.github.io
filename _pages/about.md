Welcome to my website! I'm Florian Poydenot, a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Hamburg in the Clouds and Convection group led by Raphaela Vogel, working on cloud microphysics and observations. 

Prior to that, I did my PhD at the Laboratoire de Physique de l'ENS under the supervision of Bruno Andreotti on the coalescence growth of cloud droplets.
